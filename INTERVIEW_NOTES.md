# BluBridge Interview Notes

## 30-second project explanation
I built an AI-powered B2B Lead Generation and CRM Assistant. It helps organize prospects, score leads based on company size, decision-maker role and potential AI requirement, suggest AI business use cases, generate personalized cold emails and track leads through a sales pipeline.

## Why I built it
The project was designed to understand how AI can support business development rather than only building a technical AI model.

## Important concepts to know
- B2B: Business-to-business sales.
- Lead: A potential business contact.
- Qualified lead: A prospect that has business relevance and a realistic opportunity.
- Pipeline: The stages through which a prospect moves.
- CRM: A system for managing prospects, interactions, follow-ups and opportunities.
- Cold outreach: Contacting a prospect without an existing relationship.

## Likely question
Why did you not directly scrape LinkedIn?

Answer:
I kept the portfolio prototype focused on the sales workflow and avoided unauthorized scraping. In a real company environment, I would use approved prospecting tools and APIs according to their terms and the company's process.

## Project flow
Target company -> decision maker -> research -> AI use case -> lead score -> personalized outreach -> follow-up -> meeting -> opportunity.
